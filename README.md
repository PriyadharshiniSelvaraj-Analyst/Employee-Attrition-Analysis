## 🧩 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition to understand workforce trends and identify factors associated with employee turnover. Using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI**, employee data is cleaned, transformed, analyzed, and visualized to generate meaningful HR insights.

Excel is used for data preparation, calculated columns, formulas, and lookup functions, while Power BI is used to develop interactive dashboards that support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

### 1. Analyze Employee Attrition
Explore employee attrition patterns across different workforce attributes.

### 2. Understand Employee Satisfaction
Analyze job satisfaction, work-life balance, environment satisfaction, and relationship satisfaction.

### 3. Evaluate Employee Demographics and Tenure
Study employee age groups, years at the company, and tenure in current roles.

### 4. Analyze Salary and Compensation
Explore monthly income, salary hike percentages, and compensation patterns.

### 5. Build Interactive Dashboards
Develop Power BI visualizations to present key HR metrics and attrition trends.

---

## ❓ Problem Statement

- Understand employee attrition patterns across workforce categories.
- Identify employee groups with higher attrition risk.
- Analyze the relationship between employee satisfaction and attrition.
- Explore how salary, age, and tenure relate to employee turnover.
- Compare workforce trends across departments, where department data is available.
- Present key HR metrics through interactive Power BI dashboards.
- Support informed workforce planning and employee retention strategies.

## 🧹 Data Preprocessing Steps

1. **Data Inspection:** Reviewed the dataset structure, columns, and data types.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Checked for missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent records.
3. **Data Transformation:** Standardized column names and formats for analysis.
4. **Calculated Columns:** Used Excel formulas to derive additional employee metrics.
5. **Lookup Functions:** Applied VLOOKUP, HLOOKUP, and XLOOKUP to retrieve matching information from reference tables where appropriate.
6. **Power Query:** Prepared and transformed data for Power BI.
7. **Data Modeling:** Organized tables and relationships to support reporting.
8. **DAX Measures:** Created measures for key HR metrics where required.

## 📊 Analysis and Visualizations

The Power BI dashboard can include the following visualizations, depending on the measures and fields available:

- **KPI Cards:** Total employees, attrition count, and attrition rate.
- **Bar Charts:** Attrition by department, age group, or tenure.
- **Donut Charts:** Attrition distribution by employee category.
- **Line Charts:** Workforce or attrition trends over time, if date data is available.
- **Column Charts:** Comparison of salary, satisfaction, and attrition categories.
- **Slicers:** Interactive filtering by department, age group, tenure, and other available attributes.
